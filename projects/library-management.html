System Architecture

Key Capabilities

Book Catalog & Inventory

Comprehensive cataloging of physical books, copies availability, genre indexing, ISBN registration, and author metadata search.

Loan & Issuance Tracking

Automated book issue/return workflows, due date tracking, overdue fine calculation, and active loan dashboards for librarians.

Online Reservation Queue

Enables members to reserve currently borrowed books online, queuing pending requests and sending automated notification upon return.

Member & Admin Portals

Dual-role security permissions separating member dashboard activities from full librarian administrative controls.
